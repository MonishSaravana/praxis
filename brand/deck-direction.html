Deck direction · for 20 Oct interview

Engineering drawing, not startup gradient.

The deck should feel like the inside of a good service manual: aluminum pages, graphite type, one orange mark per slide pointing at the thing that matters, and real output wherever we have it. Below: what I took from Mobbin, the cover, what the product looks like on the job, and four sample slides that set the system.

From Mobbin

Five sites worth borrowing from, and what to take.

Antimetal

Autonomous production software. Pale gray pages, a quiet serif headline, and technical line drawings (engines, schematics) on their research index.

Take: line-drawing diagrams for the technical slides, drawn in graphite on aluminum instead of their blue.

Mistral AI · manufacturing page

A split hero: plain headline and one line of copy on the left, a bold block graphic on the right, a small mono label naming the industry above the headline.

Take: the split layout for proof slides and the mono eyebrow above every headline.

Atlas

A robotics company's thesis page. Dark ground, faint vertical grid lines, a ring logo with tiny labels, and a short argument about intelligence moving into machines.

Take: the visible grid on dark section dividers. Ours is the logo's own 64-unit construction grid.

Robot.com

One product shot on flat gray, two short sentences, nothing else on the screen.

Take: the restraint. One image or one number carries a slide; copy stays to a sentence or two.

Fauna Robotics

A big headline whose letter counters are filled with colored shapes.

Take, carefully: it confirms the orange i-dot idea works at headline scale. Don't add more shapes; the one dot is enough.

Skip

The trending SaaS heroes: soft gradients, rounded UI screenshots in browser chrome, logo walls. They read as software for office workers, which is the wrong signal for a maintenance pitch.

Rules for every slide
01

The headline is a full sentence that states the claim. Someone skimming headlines alone gets the whole pitch.

02

One orange thing per slide: the number, the dot or the word you want them to look at.

03

Every number has a mono source line in the footer, taken from CLAIMS.md.

04

Real output carries a "Real model output" tag naming the dataset. The concept video carries "Concept".

05

Images are first-person trades work only. Real footage when we have it, stock tagged "Concept mock" until then. No people-at-desks stock, no icon grids.

06

Light slides carry the story. Graphite is for section dividers and the demo video.
