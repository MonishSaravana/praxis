# Deck v2.15: changes since v2.14

- **Glasses only.** Every phone and tablet reference to Praxis is gone, on the slides and in the speaker notes.
  Competitors that run on phones are still described that way.
- **One term for who pays.** "New tech" is the person being coached, who holds a $200-a-month seat. "Every tech" is
  the whole team, for $50-a-month skills records. "Trainee" is gone.
- **Long game, 2028 and 2029 swapped.** 2028 is "Our own models", trained on pilot footage and running on the glasses.
  2029 is "A skills record for every tech": by then there are enough verified records for certifications to matter,
  and it leads into new trades.
