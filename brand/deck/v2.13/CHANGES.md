# Deck v2.13: changes since v2.12

Pricing is now worded as an annual subscription per trainee seat:

- **Slide 10 (How we charge):** the paid pilot fee counts toward year one. A seat is $200 a month, billed annually,
  with unlimited sessions, and it moves to the next trainee.
- **Slide 11:** the title is now "A seat is $200 a month. One saved hour covers it", and the middle stat is $200 a month
  per seat instead of $1,200.
- **Speaker notes:** glasses belong to the customer, and several trainees can share one pair because we charge per
  seat, not per device.
