## Sources behind the changes

- Median Series A revenue in 2025: about $3.3M (SVB data, via waveup.com). The 2–3× growth expectation and the
  "triple, triple, double, double, double" (T2D3) path come from 2026 benchmark write-ups. Directional, not a study.
- First-person recording contributor pay: about $15–30 an hour in the US (OpenTrain and Claru postings). The ~80
  hours for $2,000 is our estimate at ~$25 an hour.
- Most academic first-person datasets (EPIC-KITCHENS and others) are non-commercial. Ego4D and Ego-Exo4D allow some
  model development under their own license terms; check the official license before using them.
