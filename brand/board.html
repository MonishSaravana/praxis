Brand system v0.1 · 17 Oct 2026

Instructions that can finally see how you're doing.

Direction

It should look like it belongs on the shop floor.

The buyer is a maintenance manager and the user is a new technician wearing glasses all shift. Both trust tools that look like tools. So the system borrows from three things already in their world, and skips the purple-gradient AI look entirely.

Equipment data plates

Engraved aluminum, graphite ink, wide sturdy capitals. Source of the ground color and the expanded wordmark.

Torque stripes

The orange line a mechanic paints across a tightened bolt to show it was checked. Source of the one signal color: proof the work was done right.

ANSI Z535 safety colors

Green for OK, blue for information, red for stop. The product's state colors keep the meanings technicians already know.

Mark

A P drawn like a progress ring. The dot is the step you're on.

Stem

The procedure. One straight line, top to bottom.

Bowl

Progress, traced clockwise and left open where the work isn't done yet. It never closes.

Dot

Where you are. Praxis keeps a running belief about your position in the procedure; the dot is that belief. Always Torque Orange, or the same color as the mark in one-color use.

Geometry

64u grid · stroke 9u · bowl r 14.5u · dot r 6u · mitered top corner

The same shape works as the small progress indicator in the glasses HUD, so the logo and the product share one symbol.

Wordmark & lockups

Lowercase, wide, with the orange dot carried over to the i.

Set in Archivo at 125% width, weight 600, tracked −10, then outlined. The mark spans from the top of the i-dot to the bottom of the p's descender. Keep clear space equal to the dot's diameter on all sides; don't set the lockup narrower than 96px.

Before you ship it
  • "Praxis" is a crowded name (ETS's Praxis teacher exams, among others). Run a USPTO search in classes 9 and 42 before printing anything expensive. The mark is distinctive on its own, which helps.
  • The wordmark is outlined from Archivo (SIL Open Font License), so it's safe to modify and register as a logo.
  • Files live in brand/ in the repo: SVG and PNG for the mark, wordmark, lockup, app icon and favicon, plus tokens in brand/BRAND.md.
